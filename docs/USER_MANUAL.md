# Student and visitor manual

## Public website

### Choose a grade

1. Open the home page.
2. Scroll to the course cards.
3. Choose second or third secondary grade.
4. Browse lesson and past-exam metadata.

Protected lesson video/material URLs are not sent until an eligible student signs in.

### Browse past exams

1. Open **Past Exams**.
2. Filter by grade, governorate, and year.
3. Search by title.
4. Use **Download paper** or **Watch solution** when available.

External files open in a new tab.

## Create an account

1. Select **Register**.
2. Enter your real name and email.
3. Enter valid student and guardian mobile numbers.
4. Select your current grade and governorate.
5. Choose a password of at least eight characters and confirm it.
6. Submit.
7. If email confirmation is enabled, open the message from Supabase and confirm before signing in.

Your role is always created as student. Your grade and group become teacher-managed after registration.

## Sign in and recover a password

1. Open **Login** and enter email/password.
2. If forgotten, select **Forgot password**.
3. Enter the registered email. The screen always gives the same response to protect account privacy.
4. Open the recovery link from email.
5. Enter and confirm a new password (at least eight characters).
6. Return to Login.

If a recovery link expired, request a new one.

## Lessons

1. Sign in and open **Lessons**.
2. The page defaults to your grade when the profile is loaded.
3. Filter by grade/module or search.
4. Open a lesson.
5. Watch the video and download authorized material.
6. Mark the lesson complete to update local progress.

Lesson progress is stored on the current browser/device. Clearing browser data or changing devices resets this convenience indicator; academic marks remain in the database.

A suspended account cannot access protected lesson/homework data. Contact the teacher.

## Homework

1. Sign in and open **Homework**.
2. Filter by status or grade. The backend still enforces your assigned year/group.
3. Open an answer sheet.
4. For multiple choice, choose at least one answer and submit.
5. For written work, enter text or attach PDF/JPEG/PNG/WebP up to 10 MB.
6. Automatic homework is marked against the server-side key.
7. Review score, percentage, and correct/incorrect counts.
8. If an explanation video exists, it unlocks only after the submission is graded.

A graded automatic attempt cannot be overwritten. Ask the teacher if a key or result needs correction; the teacher can regrade from the current key.

Do not upload executable files or private information unrelated to the assignment.

## Student profile

Open **Profile** to see:

- Attendance rate and session history.
- Quiz averages/results.
- Homework history/results.
- Assigned group.
- Contact details.

Use **Edit profile** to change name, phone, guardian phone, or governorate. Grade/group are read-only and changed by the teacher.

## Language and theme

Use the navigation controls to switch Arabic/English and light/dark theme. These preferences are stored in the browser.

## WhatsApp contact

The floating WhatsApp button opens the teacher contact number. A teacher may also send progress reports to the student or guardian number stored in the profile.

## Sign out and shared devices

Use **Sign out** in navigation after using a shared device. Supabase stores a browser session for convenience; signing out removes it. Do not save passwords on an untrusted device.
